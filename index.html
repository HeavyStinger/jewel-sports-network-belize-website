Our Story

Jewel Sports Network was founded by David Maheia to fill a gap Belizean sports had never had filled before: a dedicated, professional-grade media platform built just for the local sports scene. Coverage existed in pieces, but not a team built specifically to give athletes, leagues, and institutions the same production quality you'd find anywhere else in the world.

Mission

To give Belizean sports the professional media treatment it deserves: telling the stories of local athletes, teams, and communities with quality, care, and pride. JSPN exists to make sure Belizean talent gets seen, celebrated, and remembered.

Vision

To become the definitive sports media network for Belize: the trusted name every league, association, and athlete turns to for coverage. Inspiring the next generation of Belizean talent through powerful storytelling.

What We Do

Professional Sports Photography

Edited photo galleries from games, training sessions, and events, delivered fast and ready for social or press use.

Highlight Reels

Cinematic recaps that capture the key moments, energy, and storylines from a game, tournament, or season.

Player & Team Spotlights

Dedicated features on standout athletes and teams, built to give them visibility beyond the scoreboard.

Live Event Coverage

On-site photo and video production across games, tournaments, and ceremonies. Full crew, professionally shot and edited, not livestreamed.

Branded Video Packages

Ongoing content partnerships for leagues, teams, and institutions, built around monthly or season-long coverage rather than one-off shoots.

Behind-the-Scenes Content

Docu-style footage that follows athletes and teams beyond game day: training, locker room energy, and the human side of competition.
